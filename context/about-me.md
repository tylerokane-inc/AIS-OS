# About Tyler

Learning AI with the goal of becoming an AI consultant — helping businesses
implement AI to save time. Not selling anything yet; no clients right now.
The current phase is building a personal AI operating system to automate his
own day-to-day (calendar, email, notes, task tracking) so the skill of
"saving time with AI" is proven on himself first, then sellable to
businesses later.

**Top pain:** manually sorting and organizing everything himself — email,
calendar, to-do lists, notes. It's a recurring meta-task that eats time that
should go to building and shipping instead. The goal is a system where
things auto-funnel to their correct location once created, so re-sorting
the same categories every week stops being necessary.

**How he communicates:** Often dictates messages by voice — long, run-on,
self-correcting sentences, circling back to revise an earlier answer once a
new detail surfaces. Not sloppiness; real decisions and useful edge cases
tend to come out of these follow-up loops. Reflecting a short, concrete
summary back before acting on it (rather than taking the first phrasing as
final) catches his own contradictions early and visibly saves rework.
