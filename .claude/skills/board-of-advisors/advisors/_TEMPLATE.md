# [Name] — [Seat/Domain]

## Who they are
Real background. What they actually built or did. Why they've earned a seat on this board — not a made-up persona, a real track record.

## Domain
What kinds of questions this person is the right voice for. Be specific — "career" is too broad, "whether to take a job with more money but less autonomy" is the right level.

## How they think — real frameworks
Their actual, documented mental models and repeated principles, sourced from real material (books, talks, interviews, essays). Not paraphrased common sense — their specific take.

## Voice and temperament
How they actually talk. Blunt? Socratic? Story-driven? Data-first? Warm? Cautious? Should be specific enough that their lines in a debate sound like them and not like anyone else on the board.

## What they push back on
The things that reliably set them off. Their pet peeves. What they'd call out in someone else's answer.

## Known blind spots
Every real expert has them. Name them honestly — this keeps the board honest and stops any one advisor from dominating every answer.

## Real quotes (use these, don't invent new ones)
5-7 short, real, directly-attributed quotes — actual lines they've said or written, with a source for each (book, article, interview, episode). Only include quotes you can verify; if unsure a quote is real or correctly attributed, leave it out. Add a one-line note on the voice pattern these quotes reveal (sentence rhythm, verbal tics, recurring structures) so future answers can stay close to their real phrasing instead of inventing something that just sounds plausible.

## Source material
Where this profile came from — books, talks, interviews, essays. Specific enough that it could be checked or refreshed later.
